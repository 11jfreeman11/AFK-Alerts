with Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-0.6B-Base using the approved synthetic voice 3 reference. The original
reference was generated with Qwen3-TTS VoiceDesign; no human or public-figure recording

at runtime. The generated recordings are distributed under this project's BSD-2-Clause

via audiotsm (MIT). No other voice recordings or preview recordings are included.
